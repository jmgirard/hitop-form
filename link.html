Make a study link

The link opens the form page with the instrument, the study and, when you give them, the participant, the module and the store to send responses to folded into its address. This builder keeps nothing and transmits nothing. For a Supabase table it reads the instrument from the hitop package's site, as the form page does. Give each participant a link. When they finish, the page sends their responses to the store you named, or, with none, saves them as a file in their own downloads folder.
